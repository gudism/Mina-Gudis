Atmosphere, 2025

Audio & Video

This audio video work brings together archival footage of the Los Angeles River from 1938 to 2020 with sound built from synthesizers and field recordings collected along the river between 2018 and 2021. The project moves across time, tracing the river through moments of flood, containment, and everyday use, while holding these histories against present-day encounters with the site.

The piece focuses on the tension between the river as a natural force and as a constructed system. Concrete channels, flood control infrastructure, and engineered flow exist alongside moments where the river exceeds or resists those boundaries. The audio mirrors this relationship, combining electronic tones with environmental recordings to blur the line between the synthetic and the organic.

Rather than presenting a fixed narrative, the work layers these materials to suggest a continuous negotiation between control and movement. The Los Angeles River emerges not as a stable landscape, but as a shifting system shaped by both human intervention and its own persistence.
